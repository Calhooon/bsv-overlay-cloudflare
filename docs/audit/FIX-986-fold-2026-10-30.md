### LOW-1: why the flag stays the gate

The aligned gate was built and measured: `spentConfirmed`, OR the spender's `pot_beefs.proof_verified`, OR the
spender `network_seen` AND `spenderFinal`. That is `results_sql`'s three inputs to
`is_confirmed_landing_with_proof`. With the OR in place, SQLite can no longer search a range of the partial
index, so it walks the whole index.

On the fleet fixture that index holds 8,097 rows. Every one is unconfirmed, has no spender bytes and is not seen,
so none is a landing by any of the three arms. Each `*/15` tick would walk all 8,097 entries and probe each spender
up to three times, and none of that work could ever latch a verdict.

Both rows the narrower gate leaves out are spent and unconfirmed: one D1 serves through a verified spender proof,
the other through a seen-and-final spender. Each is therefore a candidate of the confirmation pass
(`pot_spent_unconfirmed_sql`). That pass latches the verdict group under the pointer CAS on the tick it confirms
the spend (`fix986_b`).

Until that tick, the actor withholds a press that D1 shows. This is the safe direction (NOTE-4): it never serves a
press D1 does not.

The lens named one persisting edge: a stored verified proof whose fresh chaintracks verification keeps failing.
That edge stays withheld, again in the safe direction, for as long as chaintracks refuses to re-verify.

The two pins hold the choice in place:
- The overlay's pin runs the 16 combinations of the four facts. The second set is exactly the 8 flagged rows, and
  each of the 5 unflagged landings is in the confirmation pass's candidate set.
- The app layer's pin holds the three arms equal to the real `is_confirmed_landing_with_proof`, and the second
  set equal to the flag.

The mutant run is in `/Volumes/Lanes/out/bsv-low/FIX-986-FOLD/low1-mutant-aligned-gate.txt`. Under that mutant
the plan shows `SCAN pot_records USING INDEX idx_pot_spent_unclassified`, which the fleet-scan guard refuses.

### NOTE-3: why no ceiling row was added to the budget tier

No route tier reaches this scan cheaply. It runs only from the scheduled tick and `/admin/complete-proofs`, and
`make ci-d1-budget` fires neither.

The bound is native instead. The shipped statement takes the same 176 VM steps over the 2 candidates seeded into
the fleet fixture as over the same 2 seeded alone into an empty database. Its cost is therefore its confirmed
backlog's (one index entry and one primary-key probe a row), not the fleet's. The fleet's 8,097 unconfirmed index
rows sit outside the range the scan reads.

The plan is also pinned to its partial-index range, with zero full-scan steps, before and after ANALYZE. Per
candidate, the backfill adds one `get_beef` and one CAS, at most 16 a tick (`PARAMS_BACKFILL_LIMIT`).

`make ci-d1-budget` was run once to check that nothing else moved: see the lane's REPORT.
