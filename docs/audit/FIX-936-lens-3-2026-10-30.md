## What I verified in the tree

**The hook fires only for a held coin.** `engine.rs:1811-1878`: the spend
notifications loop over `v.previous_outputs`, filled by the validation's
`find_output(source, index, Some(topic), None, false)` (`engine.rs:3447`:
`spent: None`, so a coin kept marked spent by an earlier hook fault is found
again at the replay). A fault from any `output_spent` holds the transaction
unapplied (`engine.rs:1888-1896`, bsv-low #657): the coin kept, nothing
inserted, no applied row, no delete (`engine.rs:2053-2100` runs only after).
So the `transactions` row of the pot still stands at the hook, as LOW-2 needs.

**The record is hash-bound and shape-gated.** `held_output_bytes`
(`lookup_service.rs:746-816`): the spender's input for exactly
`txid:output_index` gives its `source_transaction`, else the engine's copy
(`held_transaction_beef`, `lookup_service.rs:774`); either goes through
`parse_beef` → `to_binary_atomic(txid)` (`:793`; bsv-rs 0.7.2 `beef.rs:1116`
refuses a BEEF that does not hold `txid`) → `transaction_from_beef(&atomic,
None, ..)` → `tx.id().eq_ignore_ascii_case(txid)` (`:803`), where `tx.id()`
is SHA256d of the parsed bytes. The recorded bytes therefore ARE the held
transaction, whatever their source. `record_held_output_from_spender`
(`:865-903`) then calls `output_admitted_by_topic` with the ENGINE's `topic`
and the spent `output_index` (`:878-883`): the gate is `is_pot_covenant_script`
(`pot/mod.rs:173`, the frozen HEAD and TAIL) under `tm_pot`,
`is_p2pkh_script` (`:223`) under `tm_lowfund`, the params decoded from that
output's own lock (`lookup_service.rs:257-304`). Only the spent output is
recorded. A forged source cannot reach the hook: the coin must be held, which
needs a real covenant output admitted by the engine.

**The D1 statements the heal runs** (all shipped, none new):
`store_record_sql()` (`d1_discovery.rs:2515-2534`, insert-if-absent, COALESCE

## LOW

### LOW-1 — the heal writes the pot's rows below the eviction ledger, and nothing re-evicts them
`lookup_service.rs:865-903` (`record_held_output_from_spender`), `:824-862`
(`complete_held_output_bytes`); `admit_fast.rs:90-102` (`MOVED_TABLES`:
`pot_records` moved FIRST, `outputs` second), `:665` (`shadow_move_passes`,
two passes of sequential statements, `:530` "a row that lands between the
first pass and the verification pass is moved by the verification pass"),
`:1159` (`reevict_after_write`: the SUBJECT), `:930` (`guard_landed`: the
LANDED predecessors), `:1218-1290` (`restore_twins`: a faulted table is
`continue`d, the next table restored).

Round 1's NOTE-4 judged the heal sound by the invariant "a complete eviction
moves the coin, so `output_spent` never fires for it". The fold restated it
and added that a half-done eviction "is the existing eviction-fault surface,
which the eviction's re-run covers". The invariant holds; the half-done states
are not covered, and there are two of them, both pre-existing and counted:

1. **The straddle (loop 18's own class).** A spend whose validation read the
   coin before the eviction's first `outputs` step and whose `output_spent`
   runs after the verification pass's `pot_records` step reads the record
   ABSENT (it is in `pot_records_evicted`) and records the pot again, live,
   spent by the spender, with `pot_beefs` under its txid; the ledger row
   says evicted and stays open. Nothing re-evicts it: `reevict_after_write`
   names the subject (the refund), `guard_landed` the landed bodies, and the
   pot is neither. (The engine's H2 record of the applied row in the same
   straddle is the pre-existing instance of this class; the heal adds the
   index rows, which loop 18 fixed exactly because "`ls_pot` said `known` for
   the rest of the cell and neither felt voided".) Narrow: the spend's gap
   from `find_output` to its hook (a handful of round trips) must outlast
   both passes (about two statements per table over eleven tables).
2. **An incomplete readmission.** `restore_twins` restores `pot_records`
   first and, when that copy back faults, CONTINUES to `outputs`
   (`admit_fast.rs:1260-1290`): the coin is held again, the record is still
   in the twin, the ledger stays open, counted
   `admit_fast_readmit_incomplete_total`, until the next accept or MINED push
   runs the restore again. Any spend meanwhile heals a fresh row; the next
   restore's `INSERT OR IGNORE` then keeps the heal's row and deletes the
   twin's, with the original history (`createdAt`, `firstSpentAt`, the
   verdict group and the spender facts of an earlier spend). Longer-lived
   than (1): one faulted D1 statement opens it.

Consequence: visibility and history, never sats and never a press (the
healed row's `claimable` needs a proof or a checked word, which a refused
JOIN's refund never gets; the seats' hop sweeps come from the eviction's
evidence row). Beta today counts 249 `admit_fast_reevicted_after_write_total`
and 2 `admit_fast_evict_incomplete_total`: writes outrunning open evictions
are not exotic on this fleet.

**Fold:** on the heal path only (an absent record or absent bytes, never the
common spend), ask the pot's ledger first: `PotStorage::open_eviction(txid)`
(the D1 backend `admit_fast::open_eviction`, one primary-key read; the
default none) and record nothing under an open eviction, logging it; the
pointer is then the no-op it was before the PR, and the readmission's restore
brings the real row back. Or the stronger form: the D1 note
(`note_recorded_at_spend`) hands the healed txid to the request's post-write
guard as `guard_landed` takes a landed body, so the heal's rows are
re-evicted like a landing's. Pin: a real engine over the memory store with an
open eviction stubbed on the heal path (the fold's `fix936f_l1` fixture plus
an `open_eviction` hook), RED with the read absent.

## Answers to the six surfaces

1. **The write at a spend:** SOUND. The shape gate is the admission hook
   itself under the engine's own topic and the spent index; the record's txid
   is bound by SHA256d of the parsed bytes after `to_binary_atomic(txid)`,
   from the carried source or the engine's `transactions` row, never by a row
   id or a name; a forged source cannot reach a hook that fires only for a
   held coin.
2. **LOW-1's trade-off:** the S2 and D17 shape; a FAULT-class letter, not
   `not_now`; bounded and visible; no silent hold; a deterministic read fault
   is not reachable by data (NOTE-1).
3. **The heal's reach:** unchanged by the fold; the unspent cut pot is a
   visibility residual with the money on chain (NOTE-2).
4. **Anything else moving:** nothing; round 1's check-4 premise was wrong and
   the fold is right (NOTE-3). The one gap: the heal writes below the
   eviction ledger and no guard names the healed pot (LOW-1).
5. **The pins:** they drive the production path (the real engine, manager
   and service; the shipped SQL text under rusqlite), are non-vacuous, and my
   three in-place mutants red them (NOTE-8); the D1 adapter itself is driven
   by no pin (NIT-1).
6. **The budget:** the tier has no cell on this path, so its ceilings stand;
   production pays one primary-key read per spent recorded coin (NOTE-4).
