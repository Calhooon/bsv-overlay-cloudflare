## 1. Money

**(a) Can the non-final word admit or latch? No.**

- The door has three readings, each answered `Err`, which is the retryable 502 and never an admission:
  - the pre-flight probe's terminal judgement (`broadcaster.rs:2048-2055`), where only `Present { height: Some(_) }`
    admits;
  - the submit echo (`broadcaster.rs:3185-3195`);
  - the SEEN poll (`broadcaster.rs:3387-3393`).
  A mined presence is a transaction that is final by the chain's own block, so that admission is right. It rests on
  the courier's claimed `blockheight` exactly as #602's LOW-3 states for every terminal word (pre-existing).
- The pending watch's `WitnessLook::NotFinal` arm (`admit_fast.rs:2580-2591`) counts and logs. It never latches and
  never runs the evidence check. The watch's latch stays on `WitnessLook::Seen` alone (`broadcaster.rs:117-121`).
- `/arc-ingest` answers `HoldNotFinal` (`routes.rs:2975-2987`): a log and a counter, no job, no latch.
- `network_seen` still latches only on a broadcaster's SEEN. Nothing in the diff writes the latch.

**(b) Can a really rejected refund stay alive past the age floors? Yes, up to 48 h, bounded (M1).**

Where the hold ends:
- The retire pass reads `NotFinal` on its absence arm (`proof_fetcher.rs:4352-4354`, 48 h, both indexers 404). The
  1 h terminal arm is never reached for it (`RETIRE_MIN_AGE_SECS` 3,600 at `:4298`, `RETIRE_ABSENT_MIN_AGE_SECS`
  48 h at `:4301`).
- The evidence check never refuses on it (`admit_fast.rs:2085-2092` through `never_refused`, `:2135-2147`).
- A subject that mines is presence everywhere.

Why the hold outlives the lock height (M1):
- Arcade persists the peer's TX_LOCK_TIME line as a terminal REJECTED with arc code 476. `propagator.go:1763-1798` at
  `85bfe5d`: "a line here means the peer's view trails". `docs/plans/dependency-aware-dispatch.md:131` lists
  TX_LOCK_TIME among the terminal REJECTED codes.
- Arcade itself never re-validates the row. Its text asks the submitter to "resubmit after the locktime expires".
- The overlay's door never re-feeds a subject Arcade holds terminal ("TERMINAL AT ARCADE, NEVER RE-FEED IT",
  `broadcaster.rs:2962-3010`, pre-existing, the D1-CALLBACK-FLOOD rule).
- So after the lock height every live look keeps reading the stale `NotFinal`. Every re-presentation through the door
  gets the same 502, and the answer's own text, "present it again after the lock height" (`broadcaster.rs:2083-2095`),
  cannot be honoured through the door.
- Nothing compares the lock height to the chain's tip.

What it costs:
- A refund that lost the race is still read as `NotFinal`, never as the double spend it is. Example: a settle that
  mined, or one the index does not hold. Arcade's doc (`teranode-error-surfacing.md:125`) has the finality check
  wrapping in the validator, which makes it plausible that the peer reports the lock time before the spend; that
  ordering is not verified here.
- Such a refund keeps its PENDING admission and its pot's unconfirmed spender pointer for 48 h. Under the base the
  evidence check evicted it on Arcade's own fatal word (no age floor) within seconds, or the retire pass did at 1 h.
- A presenter that relies on the door alone retries a 502 until the refund mines elsewhere. If it never mines, the
  retries never end: the owner's retry-forever smell. Under the base an aged absence answered the client-terminal 422.
- No money moves. Wallet credit needs a landing proof, Bitcoin pays the pot once, the money views are witness-gated
  and the latch is unwritten.

What the base did on the loop 44 shape: it evicted on Arcade's live REJECTED plus both indexers absent
(`evidence_verdict`'s Fatal arm, `admit_fast.rs:2100-2124`), which is the bug #836 names.

**M1 fold, four parts:**
1. Read the lock height from the SUBJECT'S OWN BYTES, never from the text: nLockTime below 500,000,000, with at least
   one input's nSequence below `0xffffffff`. That is Arcade's own `finality` rule (`finality/finality.go`), on bytes
   the door holds (the BEEF) and the passes hold (the stored row or BEEF).
2. Honour `NotFinal` only while `chaintracks tip + 1 <= nLockTime + LAG`, where LAG is a named constant of at least
   24 blocks (loop 44 measured 13). Past that bound the word is Arcade's fatal word again: the base's two-source path
   with #513's floor. The eviction it allows is reversible by the MINED proof.
3. On a probe that reads `NotFinal` past the lock height, re-feed Arcade the subject once, subject only, without
   callback registration, as Arcade's own text asks.
4. Pin each part RED first: the frozen vector with a chain tip past `972185 + LAG`; a body whose text names
   TX_LOCK_TIME but whose subject is final by its bytes.

**(c) Is the reading on the text or on a structured code? On the text.**

- The reading is a substring `find` of `"TX_LOCK_TIME (35)"` anywhere in `extraInfo`, under a REJECTED only
  (`door_words.rs:212-231`, `:237-239`). The reason is scrubbed through `scrub_already_known_tokens`, as #519 does.
- Arcade's own classifier keys on the line's HEAD token (`classifyFailureLine`, `propagator.go:1790-1798`). The
  substring match is wider than that.
- The structured code exists. Arcade's 476 rides the body's `status`, per the toolbox vector's REJECTED word:
  "the ARC code rides in status (... 476 non-final and retryable)". The overlay does not read it. The nLockTime in
  the subject's bytes is the stronger reading (M1).

**(d) Can a stranger plant the phrase?**

- **The live surfaces, no.** The probe, the echo, the poll, the live look of the evidence check and of the retire
  pass all carry Arcade's own answer, not a caller's.
- To get a TX_LOCK_TIME row in Arcade at all, the transaction itself must be non-final to Arcade's peer: its txid
  commits its nLockTime.
- Arcade's intake gate refuses a transaction non-final against ARCADE'S OWN tip synchronously, and persists no row
  for it (`handlers.go:860-882`). So a TX_LOCK_TIME row exists only inside the peer-lag window around a real lock
  height.
- **The push surface, yes (L1).** The bearer is the public subject txid.
  - A push of REJECTED with the phrase is `HoldNotFinal`: that push's evidence check is skipped.
  - The push is still recorded in `arc_terminal` (`routes.rs:3014-3021`). The record is `INSERT OR IGNORE`
    (`ops.rs:1287`), so the FIRST push's text stays the recorded word for good.
  - During an Arcade fault, `fold_arcade_look` fills the look from that record, and the new
    `Recorded`-and-lock-time arm (`admit_fast.rs:2088-2092`) never refuses. Under the base, an AGED subject with both
    indexers absent took the absence-based refusal there (#740).
  - What bounds it: the plant cannot cause an eviction or an admission, and it acts only while Arcade faults. The
    live word decides once Arcade answers. The retire pass's Recorded arm (48 h, `proof_fetcher.rs:4340-4346`) is
    unchanged, and the conflict pass (#602) still evicts a pot whose competitor is confirmed.
- **L1 fold:**
  1. `callback_action_with` routes the lock-time push to `EvidenceCheck`, never `HoldNotFinal`. The live look then
     reads `NotFinal` for a real one and refuses nothing, so the plant buys nothing.
  2. Delete the Recorded-NotFinal arm. A recorded word is the public bearer's, and #740's age floor already protects
     a young refund, which the loop 44 refunds were.

## 4. UTXO_NON_FINAL (71) not read (L2)

- Arcade maps UTXO_NON_FINAL to the same 476 retryable family as TX_LOCK_TIME (`propagator.go:1796`). The overlay
  reads it as `Fatal` everywhere: the evidence check evicts on Arcade's live word plus both indexers absent, with no
  age floor, which is the base's loop 44 behaviour.
- Today that is an eviction on it, if it ever arises. Arcade's doc (`teranode-error-surfacing.md:125`) says a lock-time
  failure renders as TX_LOCK_TIME, "the innermost wins". UTXO_NON_FINAL is then the spend of an output whose own
  transaction is not final. No honest LOW path spends such an output before it mines: every hop, settle and refund
  spends a mined or final parent.
- An eviction on it is reversible by the MINED proof (`readmit_if_evicted`).
- Not a HIGH. LOW, folded into M1: read the 476 family by its code, under the chain bound.
