## 6. Root cause per family

**A, the collected filing.** The compare's D1 side is `owed_rows` as the `owed-v3` pass left them at 05:00Z; the
collect pass filed 36 collected markers at 05:41Z to 05:52Z; the actor applied each (the records door's direct
call and frame, the `COLLECTED_INSERT` trigger) and retired the row; D1 could not (no judge in Phase C). A
compare that reads a stored D1 row against a live actor is wrong whenever anything was written after the row.

**B, a new game.** L54's games (06:53Z to 07:54Z) were judged by the actor alone; D1's `owed_rows` have no row
because no D1 judge ran. The reconcile in Phase C compares the actor against its own re-apply, never against D1,
so no Phase C process notices D1's `owed_rows` drifting; the operator route and the fleet compare do, and read it
as the actor's fault.

**C, the hop sweep.** The same as A for `hopsweep_records` and the hop's spend: filed and mined after the snapshot.

**D, the settle without a stored verdict.** A pre-signed refund broadcast at the recovery height (not through the
overlay's door) is found by the hourly discovery pass, which marks the spend without a verdict
(`proof_fetcher.rs:2394`); the confirmation pass proves it and stores the spender's BEEF in `pot_beefs`; the
janitor's backfills skip a decoded row. The judge's results view then classifies per request from
`hex(sb.beef)` (`results.rs:2692`, `classify_from_columns`, `results.rs:210`: it needs the spender's raw
transaction for the pot input's sequence and the outputs). D2-ACTOR-B MEDIUM-3 substituted `txfacts` for the
judge's explicit `tx_facts` reads (`hydrate.rs:833`, `:913`, `:1001`) but not for the results view's two BEEF
columns (`fundingBeef`, `spenderBeef`), and `TxFacts` carries inputs as outpoints and outputs as (vout, pkh, sats,
pot_lock) only, no sequence and no lock time, so it could not feed the classifier as it stands. The actor's
judgement of such a pot is `unresolved` by construction; the actor-against-D1 digest (`compared_facts`) would name
it, but the per-event compare runs only in Phases A and B and the Phase C reconcile has no D1 side.

## 7. THE FIX (for a code lane)

1. **The operator route judges D1 fresh (the compare's premise).** `rows_lever` (`object.rs:1712`) runs the D1
   judge over the identity's games with `judge_subjects_acked_to(.., ack_bound 0)` (`owed_judge.rs:685`, the
   FOLD-13 reconcile's fresh path, acknowledging nothing) into a scratch set and compares THAT with the actor,
   serving the stored rows beside it as `d1Stored` with their `judgedAtMs`; in Phase C the stored rows are
   labelled stale on the answer (`d1Rows.stale: true` when `judgedAtMs` precedes the last Phase C flip or when
   `OWED_D1_JUDGE` is off). `compare-986-fleet.sh` and the #986 issue text then read a judgement, not a snapshot.
2. **The actor can classify a spend without a stored verdict.** Either (a) the results view's `spenderBeef` and
   `fundingBeef` columns are served from `txfacts` in the mirror and `classify_from_columns` takes a decoded form
   (extend `TxFacts` with each input's `sequence`, the transaction's `lock_time` and each output's script or its
   covenant-shaped class, the fields `classify_covenant` and `settle_signers_for_spend` read), decoded once at the
   copy as MEDIUM-3 does for the explicit reads; or (b) the discovery pass's spend write and the confirmation pass
   latch the VERDICT GROUP when the spender's BEEF is held (`mark_verdict_for_spender` with
   `classify_covenant` over the parsed spender, as `ls_pot`'s hook does at the door), and the janitor's
   params-backfill takes `verdict IS NULL AND spent = 1 AND spentConfirmed = 1` rows beside `paramsDecoded = 0`,
   so the stored column is the one source both judges read. (b) also heals the 27 live pots on beta and the two
   games above without a re-bootstrap; (a) makes the actor independent of the janitor. Do (b) first, (a) for
   parity.
3. **A differential pin: the actor's facts for a game equal the D1 read's by construction.** Over the frozen
   fixtures plus one fixture of a discovery-found spend (a covenant pot, `paramsDecoded 1`, `verdict NULL`, the
   spender's `pot_beefs` row with `proof_verified 1`, no result marker), apply the game in the actor and judge it in
   D1 at one tip and assert `rows_digest` equality; RED today on the fixture's `outcome unresolved` against
   `refund`. Add the same shape for a hop-sweep filing (class C) and a collected filing (class A) as controls.
4. **The fleet compare as a standing operator route and a loop invariant at zero.** `GET
   /internal/owed-actor-compare?after=` pages the identities (the reconcile's four-seek union), judges D1 fresh
   (item 1) and the actor's stored rows at one tip, and answers `{compared, diffs: [{identity, outpoint, fields}],
   tipLag, staleD1, next}`; the L5x invariants file gets `owed-actor-judge-diffs: 0` read from it after each
   window's legs, beside `reconcileDiffs`. The compare at the fold replaces `compare-986-fleet.sh`.
5. **The re-bootstrap after the fix.** Item 2(a) changes what an actor stores for the same record: move
   `owed_actor::JUDGE_VERSION` (`owed_actor.rs:4237`, `fold11-2026-10-29`), which re-judges every actor at its
   next event or lever call under the bootstrap pace (FOLD-10 item 8, FOLD-11 L2) and runs the D1 fleet re-judge
   (FOLD-13) once `OWED_D1_JUDGE` is back on for a window; item 2(b) alone needs no version move (the verdict rows
   land as `pot` events and each actor applies them).
6. **The cutover doc's reading rule.** Until item 1 lands, the window and any one-identity read treat a route
   `diff` whose D1 row's `judgedAtMs` precedes the Phase C flip as `stale-d1` (the doc already says this for a
