## 2. Question 1, MONEY, commit by commit

### #598: can the word hide a refund that IS owed, and what re-judges it

**On this tip the word is never written, so the rule neither hides nor withholds anything (HIGH-1).** The served word is `tx_words.word` (`stored.rs:13` UPSERT; `Row::view`, `Row::rechecked`), and a stored word is `served::fields(state.word())` (`served.rs:15-16`). In bsv-tracker 0.7.0 `Word::Rejected` is produced only by `on_verdict` (`state.rs:518`, `Evidence::Verdict`) and `Word::Conflicted` only by `on_competitor` (`state.rs:392`, `Evidence::Competitor`) or `on_verdict`; `on_hint` (`state.rs:324`) never moves a word to either, and `on_chain` (`state.rs:471`) yields `Stale` or a recheck. The app layer steps `Input::Hint`, `Input::Host`, `Evidence::Proof`, `Evidence::Recheck` and `Evidence::Chain` only (`runtime.rs:525-697`, `chain_events.rs:323`); a grep of `crates/low-app-layer/src` for `Evidence::Verdict`, `Evidence::Competitor` and `NodeVerdict` finds nothing. The overlay ships five kinds (`seen` `ops.rs:1113`, `accepted` `routes.rs:1757`, `submit` `:2512`, `callback` `:3175`, `proof` `proof_fetcher.rs:1205`); an Arcade REJECTED on a `callback` becomes a terminal HINT (`terminal_hint`, `runtime.rs:937`) recorded as `legacy_body.trackerTerminal` (`runtime.rs:695`) with the word kept, exactly as NS-3 states ("without proof the word is kept, the terminal mark is set"). The eviction's `rejected` or `conflicted` (#621) is the overlay's served `evicted.word` on its own views, never a `tx_words` row, and an evicted pot has no owed row (#518). So `refused_fundings` is always empty in production, and the loop-22 shape the issue names (a SEEN-latched JOIN Arcade calls REJECTED, the pot unevicted) still serves `present-refund`, claimable, every press a refused broadcast.

**Had a producer existed, the readmit cases.** The UPSERT keeps a chain-tier row against every hint-tier write (`stored.rs:13`: a `seen` cannot replace a `rejected`) and `project` keeps an old chain row unless the state is `Mined` (`stored.rs:205`); `DUE` never refreshes a `rejected` row (`stored.rs:249`) and `SWEEP` never removes one with a `transactions` row (`:244`). So a pot evicted with the word `rejected` and readmitted by a fresh accept at the door (#513) would keep `fundingHeld: seen` (the latch restored by the shadow move) and the word `rejected` until a checked proof lands: the `proof` event `verify_bump_detailed` queues unconditionally (`proof_fetcher.rs:1205`) after `readmit_if_evicted` on the MINED push (overlay `routes.rs:3338`; the `callback` event at `:3175` is dropped by `note_held` for a still-evicted pot), at which the hide ends. A refund is pressable only past its recovery gate, after the pot's block, so the hide would end before the press is possible; `checked_tracker_mined` rescues first in the arm. That is the design the fold must keep (HIGH-1's (a) and (b)).

**The tier.** `funding_refusal_word` reads `word` alone though its doc says chain-tier; a hint-tier `rejected` does not exist in the tracker's vocabulary today, so this is a pin to add, not a fault (HIGH-1 (c)).

### #608: can the re-judge serve a press on a row the chain contradicts

No. The mark sits only on `Unbound` hop rows with `claim: null` (`owed.rs:3937-3960`) and on the faulted-read arm; `should_recompute` only decides WHEN the next read walks, and the walk is the whole `owed_recompute_inner` under every rule the base has (the index's latch first, #736; the chain rung as a downgrade, #669; FIX-837's belt). A re-judge can serve nothing a recompute could not. The cost is LOW-1: the stored-bytes reads are 16 a pass, newest first, persisted once read, so that tail converges over passes (`routes.rs:4799-4918`); the courier asks are 2 a pass (`OWED_SPENDER_COURIER_READS_PER_RECOMPUTE`, `routes.rs:5035`), and a spender no courier serves is unread every pass, so that identity now walks on its first read past 61 s instead of 301 s, each walk 12 to 26 s at fleet scale (#837), two courier asks each, bounded by #499's ceiling and #800's hand-off and by nothing new.

### #567: what the client reads under the 20 s settle and the fleet's load

The wait is paid by ONE read per refresh: the read whose `owed_refresh_begin` claim was admitted under the ceiling (`routes.rs:7163-7190`). A shed read, a read whose claim another holds and a read the ceiling refuses never wait; a read under a first snapshot computes inline as before. So the cost is at most 2 s on that read, zero on the rest, never 2 s on every refreshing read. On a heavy identity (12 to 26 s walks) that read pays the whole 2 s and still answers the stored rows, so the over-report after idle (the issue) is fixed exactly where the walk fits the wait (the issue's 244 ms); the lane says unmeasured on beta (LOW-2). The `refresh`/`retryAfterMs` body marks are untouched (a shed read never enters the race).
