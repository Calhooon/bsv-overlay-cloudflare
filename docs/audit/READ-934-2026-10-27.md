# READ-934: what the app layer's `POST /internal/tx-word` waits on for about 100 s (bsv-low #934)

Lane READ-934, 2026-10-27, branch `lane/READ-934` from the overlay's main eaaee0ec. Loop 52's class S2 (the overlay's
word park stuck at its cap, every frame exhausted). Read-only reads of `low-app-layer-beta`'s, `low-overlay-beta`'s
and `rust-chaintracks`' Workers events, 17:54:30Z to 18:03Z, and of beta's D1 (`low-overlay-db-beta`) at about 19:30Z.
Verdict: cause found; fixed in this lane with its pin (section 4).

## 1. The answer

The handler waits on its own inline owed judgement: `event_held` wrote the word and then ran `judge_word_write`'s
drain (up to sixteen marked subjects, one after another, each a hydration of sequential D1 reads and CHAINTRACKS root
checks) before `ingest` built its answer; the overlay starts all 128 due frames of a drain pass at once, so 121 such
judges ran together, each waiting on I/O (CPU 32 ms median over a 100 s wall), and none answered before the overlay's
`/admin/complete-proofs` invocation ended at about 100 s and its still-open subrequests were cancelled. Not a lock: no
Durable Object, no belt reservation and no D1 write lock is on the path, and other D1 routes answered in 250 to 450 ms
in the same minutes.

## 2. The path, end to end (eaaee0ec)

The caller (the overlay):

- `change_park.rs:497` `deliver_parked`: reads up to `DRAIN_MAX_ROWS` (128, `change_park.rs:217`) due word rows, then
  starts EVERY selected frame at once (`join_all`, `change_park.rs:525-536`), each `race_or_deadline(port.post(..),
  port.wait(DRAIN_WALL_MS))`, the wall 10 s (`change_park.rs:220`). A frame not answered `held` inside the wall is
  refused and backed off (`REFUSE_SQL`, 15 min doubling to 24 h, then exhausted).
- `change_park.rs:756-777` `WorkerUnpark::post`: `change_flush::post_body_answer` to `/internal/tx-word` over the
  service binding; an answer that does not read `held` (`tracker_events::answered_held`) is `Err`.
- The race DROPS the Rust future; the platform fetch behind it is not aborted. The subrequest stays open until the
  calling invocation ends, which is when the callee is cancelled. That is the "canceled at about 100 s": the end of
  the overlay's request, not a bound of the app layer's.

The callee (the app layer):

- `lib.rs:443` routes `POST /internal/tx-word` to `tracker_host::runtime::ingest`, after the per-isolate
  `schema::ensure_latch_columns` boot (`lib.rs:440`).
- `runtime.rs:718` `ingest`: bearer, `req.json()`, txid check, then `event(env, id, kind, body)`, and only then the
  answer `{"ok":true,"held":..}`.
- `runtime.rs:541` `event`: `live_hold` (one D1 read, `stored::LIVE_HOLD`). An unheld frame answers here.
- `runtime.rs:574` `event_held`, in order: `stored::read` (D1); `held_proof` (D1 reads); for `callback` an Arcade
  `GET /tx/{txid}` (`provider_get`, logged `[courier] arcade`); for `fallback`/`tick` the courier leg; a pushed proof's
  CHAINTRACKS headers; for `tick` with a re-ask the courier ladder; `stored::project`; then
  `chaintracks_present_height_env` (CHAINTRACKS `findChainTipHeaderHex`) and `owed_judge::judge_word_write`.
- `owed_judge/writers.rs:96` `judge_word_write`: the `tx_words` UPSERT in its own batch (durable here: its triggers mark
  every subject naming the word), then `drain_changed(.., [txid], "word")`: `CHANGED_SQL` (at most
  `OWED_JUDGE_SUBJECTS_PER_INVOCATION`, 16), then `judge_subjects` per subject, SERIALLY.
- `owed_judge.rs:392` `judge_subjects` → `judge_with_write`: `hydrate` (`owed_judge/hydrate.rs`, dozens of sequential
  D1 reads, some per hop, and per proof-latch height `canonical_root` → `beef_guard::canonical_root` →
  `internal_events::chaintracks_header`, CHAINTRACKS round trips, memoised 30 s per isolate), the pure derivation, one
  counted commit batch, then `first_party_push` to the relay per changed identity (one POST, no retry).

No serialisation on the path: the handler calls no Durable Object (the belt actor `OWED_BELT_ACTOR` and its drain are
the alarm's, not this route's), takes no isolate lock, and D1 serialises statements, not handlers.

## 4. The fix (this lane)

A word frame's write does not wait on the judge: the write is durable and its trigger marks are enough; the judge is
detached.

- `owed_judge/writers.rs`: `judge_word_write` is now `write_word` (the same UPSERT, its own batch) then
  `judge_written_word` (the same `drain_changed`); no statement changed.
- `runtime.rs` `event_held` ends at `land_word` (the write alone, `routes::D1Sql`): no tip read, no judge.
- `runtime.rs` `ingest_frame`: the answer is built once the event returned; a held frame's txid is handed to the
  route's judge, which `ingest` runs in `ctx.wait_until` under the owed drain's wall (`OWED_DRAIN_WALL_MS` 20 s less
  `OWED_DRAIN_RESERVE_MS`), exactly the record door's detached judge (eaaee0ec's `record_post::detached`). A judge cut
  or faulted leaves its marks for the drain.
- `runtime.rs` `tick` (the scheduled pass, no caller waiting) judges each landed word inline, as before.

What is still waited on before the answer: the event's own reads (`live_hold`, `stored::read`, `held_proof`), a
`callback` frame's Arcade GET (0.5 to 1.2 s in the events), a pushed proof's header check, and the UPSERT. Not
measured on beta (no deploy in this lane).

Pin: `--test owed_judge_sqlite read934_a_word_frame_is_answered_once_written_never_after_its_judge`. The overlay's
producer writes the frame (`tracker_events::note`, `drain`); the app layer's `ingest_frame` lands it through
`land_word` over the shipped migrations on SQLite whose judge port never answers (`hydrate` and `canonical_root`
pending); the call is raced against the overlay's `change_park::DRAIN_WALL_MS` on tokio's paused clock; the answer
reads held by the overlay's `answered_held`, the word is in `tx_words`, all 40 subjects naming it are marked and none
judged, the judge is handed the txid; then `judge_written_word` judges 16 and leaves 24 marks. RED with eaaee0ec's
rule grafted into `land_word` (the word event judging before it returns): `the frame is answered inside the producer's
wall: Elapsed(())`. Amended source pins: `record_post::tests::owed893_writers_use_the_judge` (the event lands and
judges nothing; `ingest` detaches `judge_word`; `tick` judges inline) and `i757_spender_word`'s list (`land_word`).

## 5. What the actor design changes (DESIGN-D2-ACTOR-2026-10-26)

Lane A moves the word frames onto the `owed-events` queue and off `POST /internal/tx-word`, so no POST and no park
remain to stall; lane B moves the judgement to the per-identity actor. Until phase C (`OWED_D1_JUDGE=false`) the D1
judge stays the writer of `owed_rows`, and the design's cutover DRAINS the parked word frames first (LOW-4, keeping the
tracker ingest's D1 write): with eaaee0ec's handler that drain cannot finish (every frame refused). This fix is the
design's rule 1 ("a write answers once its rows are durable; no judge inside the request") applied to this route
ahead of the queue, and it is what the actor keeps: the ingest's write.
