## Findings

### NOTE-1: `Some(Inconclusive)` now reads as a fault at the two bool-collapsing sites (fail-safe, unreachable)

`admit_fast.rs:2547-2551` (`evidence_check`) and `proof_fetcher.rs:4966-4972` (`retire_evidence`). The base
collapsed each courier answer with `presence_bool`, so a `Some(NetworkPresence::Inconclusive)` became `Some(false)`,
a CLEAN ABSENCE; the PR's `courier_hint` reads it as `CourierWord::Fault`. The lens's extended pin counts 160 moved
decisions over 576 pairs, every one with an `Inconclusive` operand and every one in the safe direction: 70 evidence
verdicts from `Refused(..)` to `Uncertain("a courier faulted")`, 90 retire verdicts from `Some(reason)` to `None`.
No production reader yields that value: `presence_response` (`proof_fetcher.rs:4024`) answers only
`Some(Absent)` (404), `Some(Present { .. })` (a 2xx JSON object) or `None`, and both production ports
(`RetirePort for` the worker at `proof_fetcher.rs:5498`, the evidence check's `sources.*_presence`) read through it.
So the claim "the adapter matches the old hand-written reading" holds at the fold and for every reachable input,
not for the whole type. Money: none today; if a future reader returned `Inconclusive`, the PR's reading is the
correct one (a could-not-say is never absence) and the base's would have evicted on it. No fix asked; the PR's doc
comment on `courier_hint` already states the fail-safe reading. Optional: a sentence in the site table that the
equivalence is over reachable answers.

### NOTE-2: the retire backlog's `{evidence:?}` lines print `CourierWord`s, and WoC reads `Fault` when not asked

`proof_fetcher.rs:5374` and `:5395`. Stated by the lane for one line; it is both backlog lines (`HELD on the fresh
look (..)` and `no verdict on the fresh look (..)`). Where Bitails holds the subject the base printed
`woc: Some(true)` (a copy of Bitails' answer, never WoC's word); the PR prints `woc: Fault`, which reads as "WoC
faulted" when WoC was not asked. Neither is WoC's word; the new text is closer to the truth but names a fault
that did not happen. No reader of these lines found in either repository (`grep "fresh look"` over the overlay,
bsv-low's scripts and tools). Money: none. Fix shape, optional: a `CourierWord::NotAsked`, or print the reason.

### Pre-existing, not this PR (stated for the C2 lens)

- `presence_response` does not check the body's `txid` (a body naming another transaction is presence) and takes a
  height above the tip as a mined claim; a height past `u32` reads as mempool presence. Unchanged; D1's stated
  trust of a courier's height (#602 LOW-3) and the design's S4 gap H.
- Pruned Bitails' 404 on the `bitails_presence` rung is a clean absence (`CourierWord::Absent`), one half of the
  two-source bar; unchanged since #519 ("an unconfirmed subject's presence is WoC's node alone"). FIX-834's
  could-not-look reading applies to the proof ladder's rung, not this one.

## The ARC corroborator (S2, S3) left hand-written

Sound for this PR. It is not a new two-readings hazard: since L8 (overlay PR 113) the Arcade words are read by
bsv-tracker and the ARC corroborator's (`broadcaster.rs:392 arc_verdict`, `:549 corroborator_verdict`, `:1169
arcade_status_rank`) by a local list; this PR touches neither and moves no word between them. The lane's five
answers that WOULD move under the tracker's reading are confirmed against the source:

1. `UTXO_SPENT`: the tracker's `DoubleSpend`; `arc_verdict` is `Accepted` (not in `error_statuses`, `bc:398`).
2. A `competingTxs` list behind an accepting word: the tracker's `DoubleSpend` (bsv-tracker 0.7.0 `hints.rs:129`);
   `arc_verdict` reads only `txStatus` and `extraInfo`.
3. `ORPHAN` in `extraInfo`: a refusal in `arc_verdict` (`bc:400`); the tracker reads the status word only.
4. An empty or unknown word: the tracker's `UnknownStatus` (no input); `arc_verdict` `Accepted`.
5. The echoed `txid`: `from_arc_body` checks it (`TxidMismatch`, `hints.rs:118`); `arc_verdict` cannot.

Each is an admission outcome (1, 2, 4 on the TAAL leg's accept; 3 on both), so moving them is C2's with its own
recorded-table rows, not a refactor. The hazard is the existing one, not widened here: one word (an orphan view)
reads as "no input" at the Arcade gate and as a refusal at the corroborator. Leaving it named is correct.
