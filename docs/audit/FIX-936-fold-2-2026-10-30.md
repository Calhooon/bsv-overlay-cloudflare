**Why `evictions_for`, not a new `open_eviction` method.** The lens proposed a
new `PotStorage::open_eviction(txid)`. The trait already has the ledger read
`evictions_for` on both backends. On D1 it is one primary-key statement for a
single txid, the same read as `admit_fast::open_eviction`. Its read rule,
`open_eviction_hides_row`, is the one `ls_pot spentStatus` already applies to
the same rows (#937). Reusing it keeps one rule for "is this row held under
the ledger" and adds no trait method that every store must decide on.

**What the fold leaves, stated.** The guard reads the ledger before the
heal's writes. Suppose an eviction's marker lands between that read and the
heal's `store_record`, and its verification pass ran before the write landed.
Then the healed row stands under an open marker until the next guard or the
readmission. This is the straddle narrowed from the spend's whole
validation-to-hook gap down to the heal's own few statements. It costs
visibility and history only, as before, and the read rule (#937) still hides
the row from `spentStatus` while the marker is open.
