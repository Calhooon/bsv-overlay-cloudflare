### Captain's register text (supersedes the pre-lens readiness text above)
- **The served chain word (NS-3):** the app layer embeds bsv-tracker 0.2.2. On each transaction subject and every `answers[id]`, the additive `tracker` object carries `{word,tier,height,verifyPath,source,at}`; spender subjects carry `spenderWord`, owed coins carry `coinWord`. Main's `tier`, `hintSource`, `observedAtMs`, `confirmed`, `spentConfirmed`, `present` and `claimedHeight` retain their meanings. `mined` requires a merkle path checked against the CHAINTRACKS header and is rechecked on read; a changed root serves `stale`, an unavailable snapshot serves `cannotCheck` without a verify path, after one retry. A tracked txid never extends an outpoint memo's five-minute window. Broadcaster SEEN alone writes `seen`; a courier's mempool presence is the live-race `announced` hint, represented in 0.2.2 by `Unknown` with source and time because the crate has no Announced hint status (captain's K-ask filed). The tracker promotes on neither hint. The index's verified proof FIRST decides claimable, awaiting_block and swept confirmation; a checked mined word is also sufficient, and cannotCheck never vetoes the index's proof. Wallet credit still requires a landing proof. The overlay's hooks reach the app layer through the bearer-gated `/internal/tx-word` and carry their own evidence, never a courier ladder. **no read writes a word; the scheduled pass writes them; the deploy-day backfill is that pass's**. A word-store read fault on every read route serves the legacy body unchanged with tracker fields absent and is counted by tracker_lookup_faults_total; a decoration never fails an available legacy answer. Missing rows serve `tracker.word=unknown,tier=host`; no claimability is withheld during convergence. The deploy-day backfill runs in the scheduled pass, never on a read: versioned cursor, indexed 64-subject pages, at most 64 subjects plus 64 PK lookups, up to 64 header snapshots (up to 384 CHAINTRACKS subrequests with the retry) and 64 word writes, plus cursor read/save per pass. BEEF bytes are selected only for missing proof-bearing rows; a proof that does not check against its header is skipped, counted by reason and not retried until the next `BACKFILL_VERSION`; a header fault ends the pass and the subject is retried. The app layer's scheduled pass refreshes at most two aged words through rotated couriers, with three-fault circuits; absence and inability to look are no evidence. Public reads create zero tracker rows per minute, counted. A submit writes a word only after the gated arm admits or consumes coins; ungated and admitted-nothing submits create no word event. Old stranger projections, including built and Arcade-touched rows, are swept after eight days (64 per pass) and cannot renew their age by a scheduled re-ask; a callback or acceptance for a subject the overlay does not hold writes no row; tx_words is re-askable. Retained BEEF is read as bytes through beef_limits. Hook shipment/drop/wall and courier asked/skipped/faulted counters are served on health/invariants.
