## Verdict: SOUND

Nothing above LOW. The new rule takes from the #837 carry exactly the previous rows whose subject the pass HOLDS (a hop, result or refund entry of that outpoint) and for which the derivation answered NO row. Every "no row" arm of `derive_owed_rows` rests on a durable D1 word (the index's spend pointer, `pot_spenders`, the eviction ledger, the verified `collected` and `held` filings, the `homeproof:` latch, the marker's age and verification), never on evidence the outward budget withholds; a subject whose evidence the cut withheld (a probe not made, a spender's bytes withheld by the clock or the cap) is answered WITH a row ("could not judge", `spenderUnread`, or the stranded ladder), so it was never in the carry's reach before or after the fix. So on a cut pass the rows the fix stops carrying are rows an UNCUT pass over the same inputs also retires: no owed row is hidden by the fix, and a stale press is removed. The #837 belt still carries what it was built for (a subject the pass did not hydrate). One LOW (a residual outside the fix, stated) and three NOTEs.

## How the question was answered

1. What `budget_cut` means. It is the walk's clock, tripped only in the OUTWARD phases (`routes.rs:4310` the probes, `:4925` the spender reads, `:5039` the courier reads, the pay-vout pass `:5491`). The candidate cursor's own clock is an `Err` (`routes.rs:3390`: "previous snapshot stands"), so on a `budget_cut` pass every selected party page and hop page IS hydrated; what the pass never reaches are the pages past `OWED_WALK_MAX_PAGES` and the subjects the selection quota left (`walk_cut`), which are not in `i.hops`, `i.results` or `i.refunds` and are therefore NOT in `judged_outpoints`: carried as #837 rules.
2. "The subject the pass holds" against "the subjects the derivation judged". `judged_outpoints` (`owed.rs:1934`) is every `i.hops` hop outpoint and every `i.results` / `i.refunds` pot outpoint. `derive_owed_rows` (`owed.rs:3393`) iterates `i.results` (keyed `pot_txid:pot_vout`) and `i.hops` (keyed `hop_txid:hop_vout`), each through `outpoint_key` (lowercase), the same key every derived and stored row carries. A swept hop's payout is keyed by the HOP outpoint, a pot's payout or refund by the POT outpoint, so no row family of one subject is keyed on another's outpoint. One inexactness: a refund entry whose pot the results do not carry is "held" but not iterated (NOTE-2).
3. The "no row" arms, read one by one (results: evicted pot `:3463`, a decided loss, a verified-collected sole candidate, a verified held filing naming the settle; hops: the `homeproof:` latch retirement `:3755`, a swept home whose output the index holds spent, a verified-collected or held sweep, the #837 belt and chain arms when the pot's row is served, an index-SPENT hop whose spender is a held pot `:3978`, a young hop with no age or an unverified marker `:4025`). Each input is a D1 read the outward budget does not gate; where such a read faults the arm fails OPEN to a row (`pot_spenders_faulted`, an empty retire set), never to "no row". The cut-gated inputs (`hop_chain`, `spender_outputs`, `proof_mined_spenders`) only ever turn "no row" into a row or a row into another row: a withheld sweep's bytes make `swept_home` `None` and the hop falls to the Spent arm's "could not judge" row (`spenderUnread`), which #662's `keep_withheld_payouts` then restores from the previous snapshot BEFORE step 7b; the carry was never involved (`current.contains`).
4. The money question. A dropped owed row would need a held subject whose derivation says "no row" BECAUSE of the cut. None exists on this tip (3). So the fix removes only a shed copy of a row the uncut pass retires (on the loop's shape: a `sweep-hop` press of a hop the admitted JOIN spent, a refused broadcast `UTXO_SPENT`), and hides no claim. A carried row was never a press (`claimable: false`, #837), so the fix also removes no press that should stand.
5. The #837 belt for its own case. Loop 44's lost row (the game's row gone on every cut pass) is now answered by the derivation itself (#837's belt and chain-arm `joined_pot_unlinked_row`, and the hop-spend write); the carry stays for a subject not hydrated, which `fix837_d` pins with empty inputs (unchanged meaning: its previous rows name no held subject) and `fix837f_l4` (the row cap, unchanged). Both pass unchanged on the branch.

### NOTE-2. `judged_outpoints` names refund entries the derivation does not iterate

`owed.rs:1931` ("[`derive_owed_rows`] answers each of them") is exact for hops and results; a refund entry whose pot `gather_result_rows` / `assemble_results` did not carry (`routes.rs:3657`; the results are assembled with dedup and fail-safes, the refunds from the same paired rows) is held but never iterated. An uncut pass also serves no row for it, so the carry's drop is the uncut answer (`lens859_c`): no money effect. The doc line can say "every hop and result entry, and the refund entries beside them".

### NOTE-3. `fix837_d`'s exclusions are now unreachable for a held subject

The keep window and the verified-collected exclusion of `carry_shed_rows` still bite for an UNHELD subject (`fix837_d` holds them with empty inputs); for a held one the new guard fires first. Meaning unchanged; `fix859_b` and the lens cases below are the pins of the mixed case.
