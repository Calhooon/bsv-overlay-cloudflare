## The six questions

### (a) Permanence

`FeedPassSummary::is_reorg_word` (`chain_feed.rs:407`) is `!unconfigured && state.is_some()`, and `state` is set
whenever the row READ, whatever the pass did after: the 409 arm (`:790`, a recorded stop, never reset), the
unreadable-envelope arm (`:841`, a recorded stop), the feed transport fault (`:782`, cursor kept) and the page that
did not parse (`:808`) all `break` to the trailing `write_now!()` and `out.state = Some(written)` (`:896`). The cron
(`tick.rs:598`) and the block-event pass (`tip_pass.rs:245`) read that one predicate and skip
`run_arcade_reorg_pass` when it is true. Pin `e620_d` asserts `cursor_ahead && is_reorg_word()`: the stop-gates-off
behaviour is the lane's choice, pinned.

What the stop classes are, and how long they last. By the producer's contract (`CHAIN-EVENTS.md`, "Client contract
and consumers") a reader stops at an unknown version, kind or malformed shape, or the feed's own `unreadable`, with
no acknowledgment or skip, and a 409 is "an incident the client reports". The decoder is strict: `bsv-tracker 0.7.0`
`events.rs` derives `deny_unknown_fields` on every header and event shape, so a producer that ADDS a field to any
kind stops this reader for good, as does a reorg deeper than 400 (`valid_event`), a new kind, or a journal reset
(409). The app layer's intake resets to 0 on a 409 (`chain_events.rs:109`, `:693`); the overlay stops (stated: a
replay from 0 re-judges the bootstrap fork's window and meets the journal's `unreadable` rows anyway). Each stop
stands until `POST /internal/chain-events-cursor`.

So for an extend-shaped reorg deeper than three blocks arriving during a stop, the refuted rows below `tip - 2` are
judged by nothing in the overlay. What the readers do meanwhile: the owed judgement reads the INDEX's latch first

unable to veto the index's proof, so it does not catch this; the monitor's theft alarm stands down on
`spentConfirmed` or the SEEN latch (`low-monitor/src/logic.rs:6848`), so a courier-found spender with no SEEN latch
and a refuted proof is a monitor silence. Wallet credit still needs a landing proof (the house's money truth), so the
consequence is a wrong press offered and a wrong served state, for the stop's duration.

Before and after, the same reorg. Pre-PR: D7 read Arcade's listing each pass; an Arcade or chaintracks outage faulted
the pass with the cursor kept and the next pass tried again (self-healing); D7's one permanent failure class was its

self-heals the same way; but a RECORDED stop is permanent by contract and now also switches off the working fallback.
That is Rule 6's forbidden trade, in a narrow class (a producer change, a journal reset), and it is the one finding
here that is a design choice rather than a slip. See MEDIUM-3 for the fix shape (one predicate and one pin line).

Stated: no config sets `CHAIN_EVENTS_START_CURSOR` (grep over the three overlay configs and the app layer's), so on
deploy the overlay's feed is `unconfigured`, `is_reorg_word()` is false and D7 runs exactly as before. The gate
engages only once the operator seeds the row through the lever, as the app layer's was seeded (record 855).

### (b) Selection

`selection` (`chain_feed.rs:290-321`) against the crate's `on_chain` set (`tracker.rs:160-171`) and the producer's
field meanings (`CHAIN-EVENTS.md`: `reorg.forkHeight` is the lowest affected height, one above the common ancestor;
`deactivatedHeaders` run from the prior served tip down to the fork height with no gaps; `newTip` is the new tip):

- `reorg`: `forkHeight..=max(deactivated heights, newTip.height)`. Exact. Every row a reorg can refute is anchored at
  a height in `forkHeight..=oldTip` (every proof latched here was verified against chaintracks' root at its height,
  so no row is anchored above chaintracks' old tip), and the window covers it; the ancestor's height and the heights
  above the new tip hold nothing this reorg refutes. Lens case L2 holds rows at `lo - 1`, `lo`, `hi`, `hi + 1`: the
  two outside are never examined, the refuted one at `lo` is demoted, the standing one at `hi` stands, `scanned` 2.
  The crate itself has no upper bound (`at_or_above`); the close at `hi` costs nothing here because a row above the
  new tip belongs to a LATER reorg event, which names it.
- `fork` and `split`: `height..=max(tips)`. A fork and a split change no active tip, so the selected rows are judged
  standing (a header read per distinct `(height, root)`, no write): cost, never a demotion. Wider than needed by
  design (the crate marks them suspect and re-asks); harmless.
- `splitResolved`: nothing. The crate rechecks SUSPECT words at or above the fork height; the overlay keeps no suspect
  set, and a resolution that moves the active chain arrives as a `reorg` (the service journals one whenever its tip
  branch changes). Stated: a `split` whose losing branch the overlay anchored a proof in, resolved WITHOUT a `reorg`
  event, would be a miss; by the producer's contract the tip move is journaled as a reorg.
- `invalidated`: `tip - 144..=tip` (`INVALIDATED_DEPTH`, `:68`). The crate targets the words checked against the
  invalidated BLOCK HASH; the overlay's rows record a height and a root, not a hash, and the invalidated header is no
  longer served on the active chain, so the hash cannot be mapped to a height after the fact. A row deeper than 144
  that an invalidation refutes (an operator invalidating a deep block: everything above it leaves the active chain)
  is not examined by this event (lens case L3: a refuted row at `tip - 145` keeps its confirmation); the `reorg`
  event that accompanies a tip move after an invalidation names the fork height and covers it; failing that it is the
  operator's `/internal/reorg`, as the module states. The producer does not emit `invalidated` live yet.
- `tip`, `tipAge`, `frozen`: nothing; the tip is recorded on the row.

Pre-existing, D7 the same: the window selects by the row's `spentHeight` column (`confirmed_window_page`,
`d1_discovery.rs:3203`); a row whose column is stale against its stored bump height (review L1's re-anchor case) is
outside a window that names the bump's height, until a sweep meets it.

### (c) The shared judgement

`judge_window_legs` (`arcade_reorg.rs:720-825`) is the old inline block moved into one function: the same three legs
in the same order, `ReverifyMode { demote_proofless: false, reanchor_first: true }` (`:745-746`), a faulted leg keeps
its cursor and stops the legs after it, a budget stop keeps the spenders' advanced cursor and stops the legs after it,
a missing transactions store is a fault (round 2 LOW-4), each leg's summary added to the caller's. Inside the legs
nothing moved: `reverify_window_with` re-anchors first, re-reads the stored bump, demotes bound to the judged height
(`reorg_sweep.rs:383-680`). One difference of COST, not verdict: D7 seeded the memo's canonical root from its
corroboration read (`memo.seed_canonical`), so a height's rows cost no header read; the feed seeds nothing and pays
one `is_valid_root_for_height` per distinct `(height, root)` among the rows (memoized per pass, faults not memoized).
The fault words differ only where the window is wider than one height (`window_name`).

The D7 pins still pin the behaviour, not the signature: under the mutant `reanchor_first: false` in the shared
function four D7 pins RED (`a_concurrent_reanchor_between_the_read_and_the_ladder_ask_never_demotes`,
`a_corroborated_orphan_event_reanchors_reproven_rows_in_place_and_demotes_the_rest`,
`a_spent_ladder_budget_stops_the_page_and_demotes_nothing_blind`,
`a_reanchor_needs_a_verified_proof_and_a_demotion_needs_a_refuted_bump`), with `e620_a`, `e620_g` and the lens's L4;
as the lane claimed. Under the mutant `demote_proofless: true` ONLY `e620_g` reds (a source-text pin): no
behavioural pin on either consumer holds a confirmed row without a stored bump (LOW-1; lens case L7 is that pin,
RED under the mutant with `left: (0, 1, 0)`: `no_stored_proof` 0, `demoted_blind` 1).

Both consumers on one deployment in one tick: the cron and the block-event pass run one or the other, never both;
`POST /internal/arcade-reorg` still runs D7 on demand. Lens case L4 runs the feed's reorg at 101 and then D7's
consumer over an Arcade orphan row at 101 on the same store: the second pass scans the one remaining confirmed row,
demotes nothing (`stale` 0), the ladder was asked once in all, and each consumer's cursor is its own row over its own
source. Two cursors on one EVENT cannot happen: the sources differ. The R2 lifetime totals are bumped by whichever
pass demoted, once per row (a demoted row is not a confirmed row any more).

### (d) Bounds

Per pass (`PassLimits::cron` and `::block_event`, unchanged): at most 2 feed pages (`PAGES_PER_PASS`), each a
CHAINTRACKS subrequest raced at `FEED_TIMEOUT_MS` 10 s; one tip read for an `invalidated`; 2 (cron) or 1
(block-event) selecting events FINISHED; per event one page per leg of `leg_limit` rows (200 cron, 100 block-event),
each row one `get_beef` read, each refuted row up to one ladder ask (`ARCADE_LADDER_BUDGET` 20 a pass, a stop when
spent), each demotion two writes; header reads one per distinct `(height, root)`, so up to `leg_limit` per leg page
in the worst case (a window of many heights with one row at each), with no per-read timeout (`ct_get`,
`chain_tracker.rs:117`, pre-existing for D7 and the sweep). Non-selecting events are consumed without a count inside
the two pages (at most 200, a decode each). A window wider than a page is held with each leg's cursor and advances
every pass by at least the ladder's 20 refuted rows or a full page; a window faulting 12 passes is released with its
heal, as D7's. Against D7: two feed GETs instead of two Arcade listing GETs, no corroboration header read and no
memo seed (so more header reads on a wide window), the same everything else.

The walls: the cron runs the feed inside `run_steps` under the tick's 840 s race (`TICK_BUDGET_MS`), at D7's old
position, with no race of its own, as D7 had none; a drop there skips every step after it that tick (pot
maintenance, missing spends, proof completion, the backfills, the retire pass), as a drop in D7 did. The block-event
handler has no wall of its own around the feed (nor around D7 or the sweep before this PR); a stuck header read holds
the response. Lens case L6: a pass dropped at a caller's wall leaves the row where the seed put it and the next pass
continues from it; the row's compare-and-set makes a late write of a dropped pass harmless.

`/health/invariants`: `chain_feed::health_json` is one statement (`STATE_READ`, lens case L5 pins the count), issued
inside the handler's one join (`fix838` lists it). The tier measured 32 statements on main `35ddb423`, an ancestor of
this branch; main's `65027163` adds none; so the merged read is 33 of the ceiling's 34 (`tools/lane-499/ceilings.json`
`health-invariants.stmts`), one place of margin left. Arithmetic, not a tier run.

+    use bsv_rs::transaction::{Beef, Transaction};
+    use overlay_discovery::pot::storage::PotRecord;
+    async fn proofless_confirmed(store: &MemoryPotStorage, pot: &str, height: u32) -> String {
